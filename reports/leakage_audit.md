# Leakage Audit (Gate G1)

Generated by `src/splits.py::audit_folds`, invoked from `src/train.py`; raw output in `manifests/leakage_audit_main.json`.

## Why this matters

The study uses **6,810 windows** drawn from only **30 participants**, and consecutive windows overlap by 75% (20 s windows at a 5 s stride). A window-level random split would place two windows sharing most of their signal on opposite sides of the train/test boundary. Grouping by participant makes that structurally impossible.

## Fold-local fitting

The following are fitted on the TRAINING participants of each outer fold only, and applied unchanged to validation and test:

- per-channel robust signal scaling (median / IQR)
- per-(channel, feature) robust quality-vector scaling
- target standardisation (inverse-transformed before any metric is computed)
- classical-feature scaling
- Ridge alpha selection (chosen on validation participants)
- early stopping and epoch selection (validation participants)
- the training-signal standard deviation used to scale injected noise

`results/sanity_checks.json` additionally verifies that no two outer folds share an identical scaler state, which would indicate accidental sharing.

## Test-time corruption ordering

Corruption is applied to a **copy of the raw held-out signals**, before fold-local scaling, because a sensor fault physically occurs at the sensor. The scaler is never refitted on corrupted data, and quality indicators are **recomputed from the corrupted signal**, so the gate must detect the degradation itself rather than being told about it.

## Features never used

Participant ID, file name, recording ID, stimulus/video ID, sex, and age group are **not** model inputs. Trial identifiers are retained in metadata solely to enforce trial-safe window boundaries and grouped splits.
